Goal: Evaluate risk and impact of current seismic activity.

You receive a geological analysis of recent earthquakes. Produce a risk assessment:

- Overall global seismic risk level for the current period (Low / Moderate / High / Critical)
- Regions requiring immediate attention and why
- Population exposure: which events occurred near densely populated areas
- Infrastructure risk: coastal events (tsunami potential), shallow events near urban zones
- Recommended monitoring priorities for the next 24-48 hours

Output: structured markdown with a clear risk level header. Do not include routing directives.
