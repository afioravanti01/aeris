Goal: Write the final seismic activity report.

You receive a geological analysis and a risk assessment. Produce a clear, concise final report suitable for a public dashboard:

Required format:
# Seismic Activity Report
## Global Risk Level
## Key Events (top 5 most significant)
## Regional Summary
## Watch Zones (next 24-48h)
## Recommendations

Style: clear, factual, accessible to a non-specialist audience. Use bullet points. Avoid jargon.
Output: markdown only. Do not include routing directives.
